Common questions

Straight answers.

Can this get my Roblox account banned?

No. Larpiano does not modify Roblox files, inject DLLs, or read game memory. It sends standard Windows keyboard events (SendInput), exactly as if you were physically typing on your keyboard at high speed. It operates strictly outside the game process.

Where do I get songs to play?

You can drag and drop any standard .mid or .midi file directly into the app, or open the built-in Remapped Music tab to browse hundreds of tested piano arrangements ready to play.

Why are some notes playing the wrong keys?

Different Roblox games use different piano keyboard layouts (some use shift-modified numbers, others use lowercase alphabetic keys). Open the Mapping tab in larpiano, select the layout matching your piano game, or slow the speed slightly in the overlay if Roblox drops fast keystrokes.

How do updates work?

The built-in Updater alerts you whenever a new release is live on GitHub. Just download the latest ZIP, extract it, and your songs and saved mappings stay intact.
